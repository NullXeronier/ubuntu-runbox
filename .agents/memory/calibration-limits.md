---
name: Calibration limits
description: Why tap suggestions must remain optional and why simulated timing does not prove hardware synchronization.
---

Treat tap-derived offsets as optional comfort estimates, not measured Bluetooth latency or a promise of synchronization.

**Why:** A tap combines human rhythm/reaction, input delay, display delay and audio-output delay. None of those can be separated by a browser tap exercise alone; the user may need manual adjustment after changing headphones or output devices.

**How to apply:** Keep explicit opt-in application and manual adjustment available. Tests with a simulated audio clock establish consistent judgment/visual correction, but must not be described as verification of physical headphone latency.
